Before we start

Straight answers to common questions

Have a different question? Add it to your enquiry and we can discuss it directly.

Ask a question →
How much will my website cost?

There are no published fixed prices. Cost depends on the pages, features, content and support you need. After a short discovery conversation, you receive a scope and quote to review before work begins.

How long will the project take?

The timeline depends on the agreed scope and how quickly required content and feedback are available. Your project schedule is confirmed in the written agreement.

What do I need to provide?

We typically start with your business details, services, goals, preferred style and any existing logo or content. The exact content checklist is agreed during scoping.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share the current website and what is not working. We can assess whether a focused improvement or a broader redesign is the more practical option.

Does the enquiry form send automatically?

No. This website has no form backend. The form prepares your message, then you choose to send it through WhatsApp or your email app. You can also copy the enquiry. Nothing is sent or stored automatically.
