# Enkayjay Client Acquisition Kit

**Target:** Small businesses in Ghana that need a credible online presence and a simpler way for customers to enquire.  
**Rule:** Personalise every bracketed field. Use only truthful observations. Do not mass-send identical messages or promise results you cannot support.

## 1. Clear website-development service offer

### Professional Business Website

A mobile-friendly website that presents your business clearly, showcases your services and gives potential customers an easy way to contact you through WhatsApp, email or an enquiry form.

**Best for:** Local service providers, shops, hospitality businesses, consultants, creatives, startups and other small businesses that rely on social media, have an outdated website or have no professional website.

**The agreed project can include:**

- Discovery conversation and page plan
- Design and development of the agreed pages
- Responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktops
- Business/service content placement
- Clear WhatsApp, email or enquiry calls to action
- Basic on-page SEO titles and descriptions
- Responsive and browser checks
- Agreed launch or file handover with guidance

**Commercial terms:** No fixed public price is advertised. Scope, deliverables, timeline, fee and payment schedule are confirmed in writing before work starts. Domain, hosting, third-party charges, advanced features and ongoing support are included only when explicitly stated in the quote.

**One-sentence offer:**  
“Enkayjay Digital Solutions builds professional, mobile-friendly websites that make it easier for customers to understand your business and contact you.”

## 5. Simple enquiry and onboarding process

1. **Log the enquiry:** Record the business, contact person, source, needs and next follow-up in `lead-tracker.csv`.
2. **Hold a discovery conversation:** Ask what the business sells, who its customers are, what the website should achieve, which pages/features are needed, what content exists and whether there is a deadline.
3. **Send a written scope and quote:** State deliverables, exclusions, timeline, client responsibilities, total fee, payment schedule, revision approach and launch/handover terms. Avoid vague promises.
4. **Confirm before starting:** Receive written approval and follow the agreed payment terms. Do not begin with an unclear scope.
5. **Collect content:** Use one checklist for logo, business description, services, contact details, images and any required legal/policy text. Name one person responsible for approvals.
6. **Design and build:** Share progress at agreed points. Keep new requests separate from the approved scope and quote them before implementation.
7. **Review and deliver:** Test the agreed pages and contact paths, incorporate agreed feedback, and complete the contractual launch or handover.
8. **Close professionally:** Confirm the client has the agreed files/access and guidance. Ask for permission before displaying their work or requesting an honest review.

### Five discovery questions to use immediately

- What do you sell, and which service or product matters most right now?
- Who do you want to reach?
- What should a visitor do on the website—call, WhatsApp, email, book or buy?
- Do you already have a logo, text, photos, domain or hosting?
- Is there a specific deadline or event driving this project?

## 6. Practical method for finding potential customers

### Start with three accessible channels

1. **Google Maps and local searches:** Search categories such as salons, barbershops, restaurants, boutiques, photographers, repair services, consultants, gyms and training providers in Accra, Tema or another area you can serve. Check each business manually.
2. **Instagram and Facebook:** Find active businesses that already showcase their work but have no website, an outdated link or an unclear enquiry path. Their existing activity gives you something specific and truthful to discuss.
3. **Nearby business areas and referrals:** Visit businesses you can reach in person. Ask friends, family and existing contacts for introductions to owners who have mentioned needing a website.

### Prioritise these signals

- No website, or a website link that does not work
- An outdated or difficult-to-use mobile website
- Services and prices are hard to find
- No clear WhatsApp/email enquiry path
- A business is active and already investing in its presentation

Do not assume every business needs the same package. Record one specific observation and one relevant suggestion for each qualified prospect.

**Daily activity target:** Research and personalise 10–15 strong prospects rather than sending a large generic blast. Use statuses such as `New → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Quoted → Won/Lost` in the tracker. Respect refusals and stop contacting anyone who asks you not to follow up.

## Short action plan for the first paying client

**Today**

- Publish the final metadata update through your owner-managed Render workflow and complete real enquiry tests. Confirmed contact settings and the public URL are already applied to the source package.
- Approve the service offer, quote format and payment terms.
- Choose two business categories you understand and add 30 qualified prospects to the tracker.

**Next 7 days**

- **Day 1:** Contact the first 10–15 prospects with personalised observations; ask your own network for three introductions.
- **Day 2:** Contact the next 10–15 prospects and respond to interested replies with two optional discovery times.
- **Day 3:** Make a small number of respectful in-person visits to suitable nearby businesses; continue personalised follow-ups.
- **Day 4:** Hold discovery conversations and send clear written scopes/quotes with a defined next step.
- **Day 5:** Follow up on undecided quotes, clarify scope honestly and ask for a decision or a future date.
- **Days 6–7:** Review which category and channel produced conversations, then repeat the best-performing activity. Keep prospecting even when a proposal is pending.

**Immediate objective:** Turn conversations into discovery calls, discovery calls into written quotes, and accepted quotes into paid, clearly scoped projects. Activity targets support the goal; they do not guarantee a client or revenue.
